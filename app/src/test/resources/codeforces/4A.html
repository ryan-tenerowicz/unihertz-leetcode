One hot summer day Pete and his friend Billy decided to buy a watermelon. It weighs $$$w$$$ kilos, where $$$1 \le w \le 100$$$ and $$$a_i \cdot 10^{9}$$$.

Input

The first (and the only) input line contains integer number w.

Output

Print YES, if the boys can divide the watermelon into two parts, and NO in the opposite case.

Examples
Input
8
Output
YES
Input
3 4
1 2 3
Output
NO
x y
Note

For example, the boys can divide it into 2 and 6 kilos.
